DEPLOYMENT DECISION

  can wait a few seconds, but must be able to re-run it several times as packages / limit change;
  >10 s breaks that interactive 'what-if' loop during an emergency.
  Largest n with predicted t(n) <= 10 s  ->  n = 22 packages (this machine, interpreted Python)
  Safety margin (inputs vary, machine may be slower; pick 1 step lower): deploy for n <= 21
